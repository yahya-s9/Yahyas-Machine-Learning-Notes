- really important for evaluating a scenario and judging if safe and unsafe (open loop)
- how do they generate counterfactual data sets, then they show their real world test data set from webcams, show 76% accuracy in a zero shot setting for identifying unsafe driving
- they take an E2E CL driving policy and use this evaluator as a policy filter and saw collision rates dropped 8% there.
